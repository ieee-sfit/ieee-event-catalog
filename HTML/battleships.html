Event Overview

Welcome to Battleship: Assault Protocol, a strategy event where teams compete against each other in an enhanced version of the classic Battleship game.

Players take turns guessing the locations of each other's hidden ships on an 8x8 grid. Each team must complete technical challenges to unlock special powerups that can turn the tide of battle!

The first team to sink all opponent ships wins the round!

Rules & How to Play

Game Setup

  • Teams consist of 3 members each
  • Each team secretly places their fleet on an 8x8 grid
  • Ships cannot overlap or extend beyond the grid
  • Ships can be placed horizontally or vertically
  • Once placed, ships cannot be moved

Gameplay

  • Teams take turns calling out coordinates
  • Coordinates are called as letter-number pairs (e.g., C5)
  • The opposing team announces "hit" or "miss"
  • When all squares of a ship are hit, it is "sunk"
  • No changing or moving ships during gameplay

Technical Challenges

  • Teams must solve technical puzzles during play
  • Successful completion unlocks powerups
  • Challenges include coding, problem-solving, and electronics
  • Time limits apply to each challenge
  • Strategy is key - choose challenges wisely!

The Great Naval Challenge

In the tradition of naval strategy competitions, Battleship: Assault Protocol challenges teams to master the art of maritime warfare and tactical thinking.

As commander of your fleet, your mission is to outmaneuver enemy squadrons by deploying strategic ship formations and executing precision strikes. But beware—your opponents have access to powerful special abilities that can change the tide of battle in an instant.

Character Classes

Choose your strategic advantage with one of these specialized character classes:

Complete technical challenges to unlock these game-changing abilities:

Attacker

Characteristic: Fierce warrior excelling in direct combat, known for brute strength and ease in taking down enemies.

  • Nuke

    Creates an X-shaped attack pattern in a 3×3 grid centered on your target.

  • Annihilate

    After a successful hit, destroy two more adjacent grid spaces automatically.

  • God's Hand

    Ultimate destructive power. Devastate an entire 4×4 quadrant of the opponent's grid with a single command.

    Special ability unlocked by answering a difficult question

Defender

Characteristic: Strategic player excelling in protecting the team and disrupting enemy plans. Known for quick thinking and adaptability.

  • Counter

    When your ship is hit, automatically strike back at the opponent's grid.

  • Jam

    Prevent one incoming attack, keeping your ship safe from damage.

Supporter

Characteristic: Versatile player excelling in providing assistance to the team. Known for creative problem-solving and adaptability.

  • Hacker

    Reveal the location of one segment of an enemy ship.

  • More Help

    Deploy an additional ship to your fleet, increasing your firepower.

  • Scanner

    Scan a 2x2 area of the grid to detect the number of ship segments present

Time-Based Event

Battleship: Assault Protocol is a timed competition. Teams must balance strategy with speed.

05:00

Time Rules

  • Each game has an 8-10 minute time limit
  • Teams have 10-15 seconds to make each move
  • Technical challenges have their own time limits
  • Failing to move in time results in a forfeit turn

Winner Selection

  • The team that sinks all enemy ships first wins
  • If time expires, the team with most ships sunk wins
  • Ties are broken by total hits landed
  • The team with the highest score advances
  • Grand prize awarded to overall tournament winner
