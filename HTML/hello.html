Attacker

Characteristic: Fierce warrior excelling in direct combat, known for brute strength and ease in taking down enemies.

  • Nuke

    • Launches a powerful explosive that deals massive damage to enemies in a large area.

  • Annihilate

    • Unleashes a devastating attack that destroys all enemies in a straight line.

Defender

Characteristic: Strategic player excelling in protecting the team and disrupting enemy plans. Known for quick thinking and adaptability.

  • Counter

    • Sets up a defensive barrier that blocks incoming attacks and deals damage to enemies that come into contact with it.

  • Jam

    • Disrupts enemy communication and prevents them from using their abilities for a short period.

Supporter

Characteristic: Versatile player excelling in providing assistance to the team. Known for creative problem-solving and adaptability.

  • Hacker

    • Hacks into enemy systems and disables their abilities for a short period.

  • More Help

    • Calls in reinforcements to assist the team in battle.

  • Scanner

    • Scans the battlefield and provides valuable information to the team.
